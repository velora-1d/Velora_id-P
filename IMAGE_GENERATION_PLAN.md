## 1. ATURAN DESAIN & VISUAL GUARDRAILS (MUTLAK)

1. **STRICT ZERO PURPLE (Tanpa Warna Ungu)**:
   - Sesuai preferensi user: *“Tidak suka warna ungu — prefer biru”*.
   - Palet warna wajib: **Deep Navy (`#070C18`)**, **Slate (`#0F172A`)**, **Electric Blue (`#2563EB`)**, **Cyan (`#06B6D4`)**, aksen **Emerald Green (`#10B981`)**, dan **Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`)**. Dilarang keras memunculkan ungu, magenta, atau violet.
2. **VISUAL FINGERPRINTING (Anti-Duplikasi)**:
   - Setiap portofolio memiliki sudut kamera (*camera angle*) yang berbeda:
     - *Isometric 3D perspective* (VeraPay, Santrix Suite, POS-Skillage)
     - *Direct front flat mockup* (One-Subscribe, Velora Jobs, Data-Analyst)
     - *Dual-device split* (Santrix Platform, VeloraBlast)
     - *Close-up macro telemetry* (ISP-Jabbar23, RAG-AI)
   - Setiap kategori memiliki aksen domain unik:
     - Fintech: Blue Neon + Cyan Matrix
     - ISP & Networking: Midnight Blue + Green Active LEDs
     - Pesantren & Edukasi: Deep Navy + Warm Subtle Gold / Emerald
     - Retail & POS: Royal Blue + High-Contrast Slate White
     - Human Avatars: Foto studio asli dengan pakaian profesional Indonesia (batik, koko/peci, hijab modis, kemeja kerja)
3. **ASPECT RATIO STANDAR**:
   - Portofolio & Produk: **16:9** (High-res landscape untuk hero web & card)
   - Blog Posts: **16:9** (Hero banner)
   - About Studio: **16:9** (Studio photo atmosphere)
   - Avatar Testimoni: **1:1** (Square portrait headshot)
